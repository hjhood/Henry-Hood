Message types and frequency

Messages are limited to:

  • Replies to texts the owner sends, such as confirming a new assignment or listing what is due this week.
  • A short morning summary of the day's calendar.
  • Reminders for upcoming events and assignment work sessions.
  • Check-ins asking whether a work session was finished.

Example messages:

  • "Added: Gatsby essay, due Friday, 4 hours. Heads up, that week is tight."
  • "Reminder: dentist appointment at 3:00 PM."
  • "Your work session on the lab report just ended. Did you finish?"

Message frequency varies with the owner's schedule and is typically a few messages per day. Message and data rates may apply. No promotional or marketing messages are ever sent.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to any message to stop all messages. Reply HELP for help, or use the contact details below. Messaging resumes only if the owner texts the number again and asks to restart.

Privacy policy

What is collected
The owner's mobile phone number, the content of text messages sent to and from the assistant, and the calendar events and assignment details the owner asks it to manage.
How it is used
Only to run the assistant: reading messages, updating the calendar, tracking assignments, and sending replies and reminders. It is not used for advertising or profiling.
Service providers
To operate, messages pass through a small number of providers acting on the owner's behalf: Twilio delivers text messages, Anthropic's language model interprets messages and writes replies, and Google hosts the calendar and the spreadsheet where assignments and recent conversation history are stored. Each handles data under its own terms and privacy policy.
No sharing for marketing
No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party.
Retention and deletion
Recent conversation history is kept so the assistant can follow context, and older messages are periodically deleted. The owner can ask for any stored data to be deleted at any time using the contact details below.
